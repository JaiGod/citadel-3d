CITADEL 3D

LOW-POLY TOWER DEFENSE

Hold the path between the portal and your Citadel. Build ten original towers on glowing pads, upgrade each one four times, juggle farms and firepower, and survive 30 waves ending with the three-phase Iron Tyrant. Made in Godot 4.

▶ Play in browser
Free · no install · mouse, keyboard or touch · best waves save automatically

Features

Real 3D battlefield

Orbit, pan and zoom around low-poly maps built entirely from primitives, with shadows and particle effects.

10 towers × 5 levels

Every upgrade visibly changes the model: more barrels, taller plinths, rings, crowns and glowing cores.

Targeting modes

First, Last, Strongest, Weakest or Closest for every attacking tower.

Flying, stealth, armor

Wisps and Stormwings fly, Shades need detection, Shellbacks and Juggernauts shrug off small hits.

The Iron Tyrant

A three-phase boss: shields, summons guards, then enrages and fires EMP blasts that stun your towers.

3 maps + Endless

Greenvale Crossing, Frostline Pass and Cinder Forge, each with 30 waves and an endless mode.

Economy

Kill bounties, a bonus every wave and Harvest Mill farms that pay out each round. 1x / 2x / 3x speed.

Everything made in code

No imported models, textures or audio: meshes are primitives and every sound is synthesized.

Controls

BuildPick a tower in the bottom bar (keys 1-0), then click / tap a glowing pad
ManageClick / tap a tower to upgrade (U), sell (X) or change targeting (T)
CameraDrag to orbit, right-drag or two fingers to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom · WASD, Q/E, Z/C
WavesSpace starts or calls the next wave early · F cycles 1x/2x/3x speed
OtherEsc / P pause · M sound
